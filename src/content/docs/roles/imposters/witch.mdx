title: Witch
description: The Witch is an Imposter Role which poisons its victims instead of killing them
---

import {Badge} from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<Badge text="Imposter" variant="danger"/>

The Witch does not have the Kill ability. Instead, it has the poison ability.

The victim of the Witch does not die instantly, instead it will die after some time.

The Witch can still Vent and Sabotage.
