title: Lobby
description: Learn how to manage your game in the lobby.
---

As the host in the lobby, you have access to several commands to manage your game:

1. `/au start` — Begins a 10-second countdown.
2. `/au settings` — Opens the settings menu, where you can customize the game.
3. `/au ban <player>` — Bans a player from the current game. They will not be able to rejoin.
4. `/au invite <player>` — Invites any online player to the current game. The invited player will receive a clickable message in chat — clicking it will add them to the game.

## Wardrobe

A wardrobe is available in the lobby. It automatically mirrors the appearance of the player viewing it.

Right-clicking the wardrobe opens a menu where you can customize:

- **Color** — Change your player color.
- **Helmet Trim** — Adjust the armor trim on your helmet.

## Hotbar

While in the lobby, your hotbar provides quick access to several actions:

1. **Helmet**: Matches the helmet you are wearing. Right-click to open the color selection menu (same as the wardrobe).
2. **Armor Trim Pattern Item**: Matches your current armor trim pattern. Right-click to open the armor trim pattern menu.
3. **Armor Trim Material Item**: Matches your current armor trim material. Right-click to open the armor trim material menu.
4. **Green Dye / Red Dye (Host Only)**: Available only to the host. Right-click the green dye to start the 10-second countdown. While the countdown is active, it changes to red dye; right-clicking it cancels the countdown.
5. **Redstone (Host Only)**: Also only available to the host. Right-click to open the settings menu.
6. **Firework Star**: Right-click to leave the game.
